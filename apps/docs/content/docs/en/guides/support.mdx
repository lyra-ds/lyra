## Automated browser gate

The automated browser gate uses Playwright 1.62.1 with Chromium revision 1234 (`151.0.7922.34`),
Firefox revision 1538 (`153.0`), and WebKit revision 2336 (`26.5`). Interactive contracts are
validated across all three engines.

## Manual assistive technology

Every critical desktop workflow requires a Windows/NVDA record and a macOS/VoiceOver record.
Automated results do not replace those reviews. Review iOS VoiceOver and Android TalkBack when a
workflow has a mobile-specific scenario that differs from desktop.

## What support means

Support means the documented adapter works with keyboard interaction, focus management and semantic
markup; survives SSR and hydration; and honors themes, forced colors, RTL, reduced motion, and zoom
and reflow. Each adapter's documented differences remain part of its contract.

## Blade timing

Blade support is published from the released Blade artifact. A later React-contract change requires
a separate compatibility update for Blade. Blade is an existing downstream adapter and does not
block Lyra v1.0.
